Token conversion map (HangoutPalette)

TokenDarkLightWhere it lands
canvas #0A0A0C #F4F5F8 Screen background, presence-dot rings on app bars
canvasElevated #101013 #FFFFFF Bottom nav bar, chat composer bar
surface #1A1A1F #FFFFFF Cards, group rows, incoming bubbles, day pills, sheets, dialogs, pull-to-refresh
surfaceAlt #232329 #EDEFF3 Search fields, composer input, chips, "Online" chip
surfaceMuted #2C2C33 #E3E6ED Avatar placeholders, idle mic/Join/Message buttons
divider #2A2A31 #E2E5EB Row hairlines, nav/composer top borders, outlined buttons
textPrimary #F3F4F6 #0F1115 Names, titles, body text on cards & bubbles
textSecondary #9CA3AF #6B7280 Previews, subtitles, hints, timestamps, chevrons
textTertiary #6B7280 #9AA0AB Version footnote
accentSoft #A9C3FB #2563EB Screen titles, A–Z letters, links, Connect button — periwinkle is swapped for a 4.5:1-contrast deep blue on light
onAccentSoft #12213F #FFFFFF Text drawn on top of accentSoft
accentSurface #16294A #DCE7FE Tinted icon tiles in action sheets
canvasGradient #101014→#0A0A0C #FFFFFF→#F4F5F8 Splash / sign-in / register backdrop

Unchanged on purpose — these read correctly on both canvases: accent #3B82F6 (nav pill, send, badges, unread counters), danger, success, the brand gradient (buttons, logo, incoming-call screen) and white-on-accent text/icons. The three call screens (audio / video / incoming) intentionally stay dark: they render full-bleed gradient or black video surfaces with white controls in either theme.
